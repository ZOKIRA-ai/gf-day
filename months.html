Our Journey

12 Months.

12 Beautiful Lessons. 🤍

🌸 Month One

A Feeling Named Safa

Safa Fatima... kabhi kabhi sochta hun ke kuch logon ki sab se khoobsurat baat ye hoti hai ke unhe samajhne ke liye koi badi wajah nahi chahiye hoti.🥹🩷 Bas unka hona, unka andaaz, unki baatein aur unki personality hi ek alag si feeling de deti hai. Tu bhi kuch aisi hee hai jawn... bilkul apni duniya mn rehne wali, simple si, masoom si aur phir bhi itni different ke nazar ruk jaye.🤍 Tere andar jo ek ajeeb si softness aur innocence hai na, wo tujhe baqi sab se alag banati hai. Shayad isi liye Safa Fatima sirf ek naam nahi raha... balkay ek feeling ban gaya.🫠🌸

🌷 Month Two

Your Innocence

Safa Fatima... teri masoomiyat ek aisi cheez hai jo shayad tujhe khud bhi realize nahi hoti.🥹🤍 Teri choti choti baaton mn wo innocence hai jo dil ko sukoon deti hai, teri wo simple si khushiyan, tera wo bachon jaisa excitement aur tera apni hi pyari si duniya mn rehna... ye sab tujhe bohat khoobsurat banata hai.🩷 Tu un logon mn se hai jo cheezon ko complicated banane ke bajaye unhe dil se mehsoos karti hai.🫠🌸 Teri ye pure si vibe aur tera ye masoom sa andaaz hi wo cheez hai jise dekh kar bas dil karta hai ke tu hamesha aisi hee rehna.🤍

🌹 Month Three

Your Loyalty

Safa Fatima... teri ek cheez jo mujhe hamesha bohat khoobsurat lagti hai wo hai tera apne logon ke liye kharay rehna.🥹🩷 Tu sirf baatein karne wali nahi, balkay wo insan hai jo dil se kisi ko apna maan le to phir poore dil se us rishte ko nibhati hai.🤍 Teri loyalty, tera bharosa aur tera wo "main hun na" wala ehsaas bohat rare hai jawn.🫠🌸 Aaj kal jahan log choti choti baaton par door ho jate hain, wahan tera kisi ke liye sincerely rehna tujhe aur bhi zyada qeemti banata hai.💗

🌺 Month Four

Your Strength

Safa Fatima... teri ek baat jo mujhe bohat inspire karti hai wo hai teri andar wali strength.🥹🤍 Shayad duniya sirf teri softness dekhti hai, lekin mujhe teri wo himmat bhi nazar aati hai jo har mushkil waqt mn tujhe sambhalti hai.🩷 Tu wo larki hai jo nazuk dil rakhte hue bhi bohat kuch face kar leti hai, aur phir bhi apni achai nahi chhorti.🫠🌸 Tera ye combination... ek taraf masoomiyat aur doosri taraf itni mazbooti, yehi cheez tujhe mere liye itna khaas banati hai.💗

🌻 Month Five

Your Beautiful Chaos

Safa Fatima... teri ek cheez jo hamesha dil ko khush karti hai wo hai tera wo chota sa pagalpan aur tera apna unique andaaz.🥹🩷 Teri random si baatein, tera cute sa gussa, teri woh reactions jo shayad tujhe normal lagti hain lekin mujhe bohat pyari lagti hain... ye sab teri personality ka wo hissa hai jo tujhe sirf "Safa" banata hai.😭🤍 Tu har cheez ko apne tareeqe se interesting bana deti hai, aur shayad isi liye tere sath simple moments bhi yaadgar lagte hain.🫠🌸 Teri ye fun wali side, tera inner child aur teri wo innocent si energy mere favourite parts mn se ek hai.💗

🌸 Month Six

Your Heart

Safa Fatima... teri ek baat jo mujhe hamesha bohat khoobsurat lagti hai wo hai tera dil se cheezon ko mehsoos karna.🥹🩷 Tu un logon mn se hai jo sirf dekh kar nahi, balkay feel karke jeeti hai. Tere liye choti si baat bhi kabhi kabhi bohat maayne rakh leti hai, kyun ke tera dil bohat gehra hai jawn.🤍🫠 Shayad isi wajah se teri khushiyan itni pyari lagti hain aur teri feelings itni real mehsoos hoti hain.🌷 Teri ye emotional si duniya, jahan tu har cheez ko dil se leti hai, wahi tujhe itna genuine aur khoobsurat banati hai.💗

🌸 Month Seven

Your Own Little Universe

Safa Fatima... teri ek cheez jo mujhe bohat attract karti hai wo hai tera apna ek alag hi universe hona.🥹🩷 Teri pasand, teri choices, teri choti choti preferences aur wo cheezen jinse tu khush hoti hai... sab kuch itna "Safa" hai ke kisi aur se compare hi nahi hota.🤍 Tu bas bheer ka hissa banne wali larki nahi, tu apni ek alag pehchan rakhti hai jawn.🫠🌸 Tera har cheez ko apne andaaz se dekhna, apni feelings ko apne tareeqe se express karna aur apni choti si duniya ko itni pyari tarah carry karna... ye cheez mujhe hamesha yaad dilati hai ke Safa Fatima jaisi sirf ek hi hoti hai.💗

🌷 Month Eight

Your Comfort

Safa Fatima... teri ek baat jo dil ko bohat sukoon deti hai wo hai tera wo bharosa jo kisi bhi rishte ko mazboot banata hai.🥹🤍 Tere sath ek ajeeb sa comfort mehsoos hota hai jahan har baat explain karne se pehle hi samajh aa jati hai.🩷 Tu wo insan hai jiske samne apni khushi bhi share ki ja sakti hai aur apni kamzori bhi, kyun ke tere andar judge karne se zyada samajhne ki quality hai.🫠🌸 Tera ye ehsaas ke "main yahan hun" wali feeling bohat qeemti hai jawn... aur shayad isi wajah se tera hona itna peaceful lagta hai.💗

🌻 Month Nine

Your Ambition

Safa Fatima... teri ek cheez jo mujhe bohat pasand hai wo hai tera apne kal ke liye sochna.🥹🩷 Tere andar wo desire hai ke tu kuch banay, kuch seekhay, aur apni zindagi ko behtar banaye.🤍 Tu sirf khwabon mn rehne wali larki nahi, tu un khwabon ke liye mehnat karne wali larki hai.🫠🌸 Mujhe teri ye side bohat pyari lagti hai ke teri masoomiyat ke sath sath tere andar ek ambition bhi hai... ek aisi Safa jo apni jagah khud banane ka jazba rakhti hai.💗 Tujhe grow karte dekhna, tujhe khush aur successful dekhna, shayad meri favourite cheezon mn se ek hai.🥹

🌼 Month Ten

The Little Things

Safa Fatima... teri ek cheez jo mujhe hamesha smile karwa deti hai wo hai tera wo chota chota sa andaaz jo sirf tera hai.🥹🩷 Teri baat karne ka tareeqa, teri reactions, teri woh expressions jo shayad tujhe normal lagti hain lekin mere liye wohi sab se zyada yaad rehne wali cheezen hoti hain.😭🤍 Tu har cheez ko apne tareeqe se interesting bana deti hai jawn, aur shayad isi liye tere sath normal si baat bhi boring nahi lagti.🫠🌸 Teri ye choti choti aadatein hi hain jo dheere dheere kisi insan ko itna apna bana deti hain ke phir uski har choti cheez pyari lagne lagti hai.💗

🌙 Month Eleven

Your Faith

Safa Fatima... teri ek cheez jo mujhe dil se touch karti hai wo hai tera wo rishta jo tu apne Rab ke sath rakhti hai.🥹🤍 Teri duaon mn jo sincerity hai, teri niyyat mn jo sachai hai, aur tera Allah Sain par jo bharosa hai... ye sab teri personality ka bohat khoobsurat hissa hai.🩷 Tu mujhe ye yaad dilati hai ke asli khoobsurti sirf duniya ko dikhne wali cheezon mn nahi hoti, balkay insan ke andar ke yaqeen aur dil ki safai mn bhi hoti hai.🌸 Teri ye roohani si side, tera shukar karna aur achai ko choose karna... ye cheezen tujhe mere liye aur bhi zyada qeemti banati hain.🫠💗

🌹 Month Twelve

I'd Still Choose You.

Safa Fatima... agar kabhi koi mujhse pooche ke teri zindagi ki sab se khoobsurat cheez kya hai, to shayad jawab kisi ek moment ya kisi ek memory ka nahi hoga... balkay ek naam hoga.🥹🩷 Kyun ke tu sirf meri kahani ka hissa nahi, tu us kahani ka wo rang hai jisne sab kuch aur khoobsurat bana diya.🤍🫠 Tujh mn bohat si cheezen hain jo mujhe pasand hain, lekin sab se zyada ye ke tu bas Safa hai... kisi comparison, kisi perfection ya kisi wajah ki zarurat nahi.🌸 Har din dobara tujhe choose karna kisi majboori ki tarah nahi, balkay ek khushi ki tarah lagta hai.💗 Aur agar zindagi hazaar dafa bhi dobara shuru karne ka mauqa mile... to meri har dafa sirf ek hi dua hogi... ke usi safar mn phir ek dafa Safa Fatima se mulaqat ho.🥹🤍
